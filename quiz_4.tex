\section*{Question 1 \hfill 2 pts}
Decision problems
\begin{enumerate}
    \item can be framed as membership tests (e.g.\ is $w\in L$?).
    \item are associated with a language of finite size.
    \item are NP-complete.
    \item can be solved with context-free grammars.
\end{enumerate}

\noindent\textbf{Answer: Choice 1.}
Decision problems can be framed as membership tests.
The language contains all yes-instance encodings.
Membership answers that yes-or-no question.

\section*{Question 2 \hfill 4 pts}
Is the string $w=0010$ part of the language associated with the
context-free grammar below? Show a completed table following the
CYK algorithm.
\[
\begin{aligned}
S &\to AA\mid BB\mid BA\mid\epsilon,\\
A &\to AB\mid 1,\\
B &\to BA\mid 0.
\end{aligned}
\]

\noindent\textbf{Answer: Yes, $0010\in L(G)$.}
The table uses one-based substring endpoints.
Cells list generating variables.
The diagonal represents individual input symbols.
Lower entries are unused.

\begin{center}
\renewcommand{\arraystretch}{1.35}
\begin{tabular}{c|cccc}
$i\backslash j$ & $1\ (0)$ & $2\ (0)$ & $3\ (1)$ & $4\ (0)$ \\ \hline
$1\ (0)$ & $\{B\}$ & $\{S\}$ & $\{S\}$ & $\{S\}$ \\
$2\ (0)$ & --- & $\{B\}$ & $\{S,B\}$ & $\{S,B\}$ \\
$3\ (1)$ & --- & --- & $\{A\}$ & $\{A\}$ \\
$4\ (0)$ & --- & --- & --- & $\{B\}$
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

The full-string cell contains $S$.
The empty production does not contribute here.
One derivation confirms membership.
\[
S\Rightarrow BB\Rightarrow 0B\Rightarrow 0BA\Rightarrow 00A
 \Rightarrow 00AB\Rightarrow 001B\Rightarrow 0010.
\]
